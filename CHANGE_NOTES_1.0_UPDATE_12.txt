[h1]Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA - update 12: No Limit[/h1]

A pharmacy on the edge of Muldraugh. The backpack is barely half full by weight, yet there's not a single free cell left in the grid. The last bottle of painkillers stayed on the shelf: strength to carry it - yes, room - no. Now you can switch this Tetris off. And while I was at it, the update brings a new weapon reloading system, the Swap It system and new tile highlighting.

[b]New Java part: when ZombieBuddy asks about KnoxInventoryJava.jar, pick Yes / Allow, or the new things won't work.[/b]

The news in the main menu has live demos: a backpack with no limit and guns with parts you can take off and put on right there.

[h2]Grid without limit[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Two modes now:[/b] the familiar Tetris and "Grid without limit". The switch is in the Knox settings, on the "Game" page. Tetris stays the default.
[*]In the new mode the grid has no bottom: it grows down row by row as you put things in. Only weight limits it, by the game's own rules.
[*]Under the things there's hatching. Put a thing there and the grid grows under it. Hold a big thing over it and the grid stretches before you even let go.
[*]The "Compact" button next to "Sort" lifts things up and closes the gaps.
[*]Works in every container, pockets included. No compartments: every bag has one plain grid.
[*]A bag in a bag grows by weight: small when empty, big when full.
[*]Back to Tetris - nothing gets lost: what doesn't fit waits in red rows under the grid. A friend in multiplayer who plays Tetris sees them there too.
[/list]

[h2]Weapons, magazines and ammo[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Parts right on the weapon:[/b] scopes, lasers, slings and the rest are small cells in the corner of the weapon's tile and of the back and hand slots. Hover to see a part, click to take it off. Drag a part onto a weapon to put it on. Instantly, no fiddling with a screwdriver.
[*]Load by dropping: drop a magazine or rounds onto a gun on your back, in your hand or in the new "Both hands" slot.
[*]Faster reloading: magazines and rounds from bags you wear are now taken out and put away instantly.
[*]A magazine taken out of a weapon goes back to the bag it came from.
[*]A magazine checked with Shift shows its rounds on the tile until the first shot.
[*]Holding rounds highlights the guns they fit, in your hands and on your back too.
[*]The R menu: "Open All Boxes" when you have two or more boxes of rounds.
[/list]

[h2]Swap It[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Swapping items:[/b] press a slot's key - its item goes to your hands, and what was in your main hand goes onto the freed place if it fits there. If you use the Swap It mod, Knox leaves swapping to it.
[*]Shotgun in both hands, axe on your back, you switch to the belt slot: the shotgun goes onto your back, the axe into the bag, the hammer into your hand. Press the back key - the shotgun comes back and the axe hangs on your back again.
[*]Aiming right after a swap no longer leaves the weapon in your pockets.
[*]A new "Both hands" slot in the equipment column: a two-handed weapon is seen whole in it. Drop a thing on it to take it in both hands.
[*]Quick slots in the inventory: an empty one shows a faint picture of what fills it. They are rearranged with Alt, like the hotbar.
[/list]

[h2]Gradient[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tiles glow in their category's colour, brighter towards the middle: in bags, on the hotbar, in equipment slots, in the Found window and in the Viewpoint crosshair panel. The headers of tooltips and right-click menus too. Frozen, cooked, hot and cold change the colour itself.
[*]A new count plate on stacks and magazines, in the tile's corner. An empty magazine shows a red 0.
[*]Empty cells have a soft grey glow; grey pictures of broken items lost their dark edges and stripes.
[*]Rigid containers - toolboxes, first aid kits, cases, coolers, boxes - don't grow from the things inside. Soft bags grow as before.
[*]33 menu icons redrawn; "Remove barricade" got the crowbar.
[/list]

[h2]Keys, cars and Viewpoint[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shift + click on a key in someone else's key ring puts it on your key ring.
[*]A car key on a key ring in your backpack works: ignition, doors, locks. Taken out of the ignition, it goes back where it was. Single player only.
[*]The Viewpoint crosshair panel: a "Crosshair panel size" setting (Settings > Game, 60-160%), a "Key on hand" line at car doors, an "Empty" icon.
[*]Brochures, flyers, photos: the second "Inspect" is now "Look closer", the middle mouse button opens the picture.
[*]A mixed stack's list opens with Shift.
[*]Your own music from a car is heard four times farther outside, and gets louder as you come closer.
[*]Spongie Open Jackets: an unbuttoned jacket goes into the torso slot, not "Other".
[/list]

[h2]Fixed[/h2]
[list]
[*]A shoulder holster with a shotgun and no bandolier broke reloading, with errors every frame until Esc.
[*]The Viewpoint crosshair panel no longer vanishes after an error: it comes back in a second.
[/list]
